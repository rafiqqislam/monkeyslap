Common questions

Where are the settings?

Click the puzzle piece in Chrome's toolbar and pin MonkeySlap. Click the monkey icon to change limits, switch sites off, add sites, or test the slap.

Why did the first slap come after 1 minute?

So you can see what MonkeySlap does straight away. After your first slap, your normal limits apply (5 minutes a day per site to start, which you can change).

How do I add another site?

Type it in the MonkeySlap menu under "Add a site". Chrome asks your permission for that site only. MonkeySlap never sees sites that aren't on your list.

What counts as time on a site?

Time when that site is the front tab of your focused Chrome window. Watching a video counts; a locked screen doesn't. Totals reset at midnight.

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in with Google is optional and only backs up your settings and history so you can use them on another computer.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling removes everything on your computer. To delete your account data, see the privacy policy.
