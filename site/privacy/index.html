Privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026

MonkeySlap is a Chrome extension that limits how long you spend on distracting websites. It only looks at the sites you have chosen to limit, keeps your data on your computer, and only sends anything anywhere if you choose to sign in.

The short version

  • MonkeySlap measures how long you spend on the sites in your list. It never reads what's on those pages, and never sees any other site.
  • Everything is stored on your computer. Without an account, nothing leaves it.
  • If you sign in with Google (optional), your name, email and photo, your settings and your daily totals are backed up to your account.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.

Which websites MonkeySlap can see

When you install MonkeySlap, Chrome asks you to allow access to seven sites: youtube.com, facebook.com, tiktok.com, instagram.com, reddit.com, x.com and linkedin.com. These start with a daily limit of 5 minutes each, which you can change or switch off at any time.

Any other site is your choice. If you add one later, Chrome asks your permission for that site only, at that moment. If you remove a site from your list, MonkeySlap stops measuring it. You can also remove access in Chrome under chrome://extensions → MonkeySlap → Details → Site access.

On the sites in your list, MonkeySlap only notes which site is open in the front tab and for how long, so it can show the slap when your limit is reached. It does not read, store or send page content, page addresses, search terms, messages, passwords or keystrokes.

What is stored on your computer

  • Your list of sites, their daily limits and your settings (for example sound on or off).
  • For each site in your list: minutes spent and number of slaps per day. Days older than 90 days are deleted automatically.
  • The date of your first slap, and a random device ID (used only if you sign in, to keep each computer's totals separate).
  • If you sign in: your sign-in session from our sign-in provider.

This data is stored by Chrome for the extension. Uninstalling MonkeySlap deletes it.

If you sign in with Google (optional)

Signing in lets you back up your settings and history and use them on another computer. When you sign in:

  • Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with us. We ask for nothing else from your Google account.
  • We store your settings and site list, your daily totals per site (minutes and slaps) and the date of your first slap, linked to your account.
  • Your data is protected so that only your account can read or change it.

MonkeySlap's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy and the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use this information only to provide sign-in and backup.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase provides our sign-in and database. Account data is stored on Supabase servers in Singapore.
  • Google handles the "Sign in with Google" step.

We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone else, and we don't use it for advertising or credit decisions.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your computer: daily totals are kept for 90 days. Uninstalling the extension removes everything.
  • In your account: kept until you ask us to delete it. Signing out stops any further backup.
  • To delete your account and all its data, email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you signed in with. We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Children

MonkeySlap is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes
